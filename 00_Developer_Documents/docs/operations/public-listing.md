## Get Fk'd mode (full)

Get Fk'd exists for the night you already know what you want: nearby energy, right now, with another adult who wants the same thing. Not a week of small talk. Not a map of strangers. A confirm-to-enter room for people who are actually in the mood.

It is optional and off by default. Ordinary swipe, matches, and dates keep working with it off. You read a confirm screen, then enter. A Get Fk'd match happens only when both adults have the mode on. Leave and those matches and chats dissolve. That is the point: the heat is for tonight, and it ends when you walk out.

The phone uses foreground Bluetooth only. The radio carries a shared service identifier. It does not broadcast your name, photos, profile, or account. Closeness cues stay on your device. They play only while the app is open. There is no lock-screen ping and no live radar of strangers. Exact coordinates are not dumped on the public deck.
